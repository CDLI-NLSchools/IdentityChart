Identity Chart

Grade 8 STEAM, Body, Mind and Soul, Lesson 14: Identity Chart

Who you are is made up of many parts. Fill in each box below with words or short phrases that describe you. Add as many entries as you like in each box. Your chart stays on this page: nothing is sent anywhere. When you finish, print or copy your chart to keep it with your work.

My name

Type your name here. It goes at the top of your chart.

Biology

How you look: your hair, your height, your features. For example: long black hair, tall, great smile.

Background

Where you are from, and the cultures and beliefs that are part of your life. For example: Gander, NL, immigrant, Christian.

Family

The people in your family. For example: mom, 2 sisters, grandmother.

Friends

Your closest friends. For example: Grace, Eric, Alyson.

Personality

What you are like. For example: outgoing, hard working, funny.

Experiences

Big moments and events in your life. For example: moved to Canada at 4 years old, drama fest provincials, ski trip with school.

Dreams/Goals

What you hope to do or become. For example: social worker, learn how to kayak, travel to Asia.

Hobbies/Interests

What you enjoy doing. For example: hiking, painting, listening to music, basketball.

Media

The apps, shows, games, and accounts that are part of your life. For example: Instagram account, TikTok account.
